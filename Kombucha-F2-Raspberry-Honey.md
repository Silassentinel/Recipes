# Raspberry Honey Kombucha F2

## Ingredients:
- 500 mL Ceylon black tea F1 kombucha (older batch, F1 complete)
- 50–150 g raspberries
- 10–15 g honey

## Instructions:
1. Strain the SCOBY and starter liquid from the base kombucha.
2. Add the raspberries and honey to a 500 mL bottle with the base kombucha.
3. Fill to the neck, leaving 1–2 cm headspace.
4. Seal and store warm for 1–4 days.
5. Burp daily to release pressure and avoid geysers.
6. Taste-test from day 2 onward; refrigerate once flavor and fizz hit your preference.
